    About AraDict

    AraDict is an Arabic–English dictionary for Modern Standard Arabic. Paste a word exactly as you found it, with or without vowel marks and with its attached prefixes and suffixes, and see every way it can be read.

    Readings are grouped under their dictionary headword, most common first. Each headword shows its part of speech, its root and how often it appears in news and other texts.
